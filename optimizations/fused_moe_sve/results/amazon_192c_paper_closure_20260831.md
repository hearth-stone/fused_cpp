# Amazon 192-core CPU MoE paper-closure measurements

Status date: 2026-08-31.

This report records the supplemental measurements used to refresh the project
status documents. It is not a frozen paper artifact. Most extension-based
measurements below use a binary built from source commit `67e2dac` with
`_moe_C` SHA256
`5ed0b9c440acbf151cfbc22d95b00b6eee13fcd977ea2a16050018ee8cce73a9`.
The canonical explicit-unfused comparator was subsequently committed as
`266da2c`. Every headline result must therefore be rerun from one final commit
before publication.

## Environment and evidence boundary

- Host: Amazon `c9g.metal-48xl`, 192 Neoverse-V3 cores, two NUMA nodes.
- Placement: NUMA1 CPUs 96--191 with memory bound to node 1.
- Main extension build: GCC 15.2, SVE128, BF16/i8mm, Xbyak AArch64.
- Main profile SHA256:
  `54d0567df21138fd1aa6bd6775105c7c88483c21f19277b769ffe3db2d2ef627`.
- Main kernel/planner comparisons: 5 warmups, 31 randomized paired samples,
  and four rotating weight copies unless stated otherwise.
- The temporary summary records 8 focused native tests passing before timing,
  but the exact command was not preserved in a committed manifest. Treat this
  as provisional QA, not the final correctness record.
- Retained raw artifacts: remote
  `/data/zhangxu/bench_results/paper_20260831`; the local working copy was
  `/tmp/moe_paper_20260831` and is not a durable artifact location.

These measurements support bounded mechanism and diagnosis claims. They do not
establish a current upstream comparison, cross-machine portability, a general
near-optimal planner, or a full-model/serving speedup. The exact invoked
commands must be regenerated by the declarative runner before artifact freeze.

### Production tile-window policy

On complete captured traces, only the uniformish 2048/4096 cases show a clear
gain: +7.23% and +4.85%, respectively. Median 2048 is -0.16%; the remaining
median/high-skew 4096 and all 30,720-token cases range from -0.04% to +1.12%.
The historical `captured DSV4 +10.6%` result is mechanism provenance, not a
general current-binary claim.

### Canonical explicit-unfused comparator

The retained Lab artifact uses the production FEXPA-plus-quadratic SiLU
evaluator and the canonical explicit `SiLU(gate) * up` dataflow. It records the
declared boundary and production-size correctness checks as passing. Output
relative-L2 was 0.215%--0.231%, with maximum absolute error no larger than
`3.052e-5`; the final rerun must preserve the exact command and raw samples.

| Uniform workload | Explicit | Fused | Fused speedup (`T_explicit/T_fused`) |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 24 experts x 4T, M=48 | 1.747 ms | 2.059 ms | 0.848x |
| 12 experts x 8T, M=192 | 1.649 ms | 1.662 ms | 0.992x |
| 6 experts x 16T, M=2040 | 8.322 ms | 6.732 ms | 1.236x |

Fusion is not a universal small-route speedup: it is 17.87% slower at M=48,
within 0.79% at M=192, and 19.11% lower latency at M=2040. This standalone
comparison excludes route construction and merge, uses anonymous
`posix_memalign` rather than the extension HugeTLB allocator, and retains
summary statistics rather than raw samples. Cite only within-process ratios.

## Cost-model measurements

The thin analytical service calibration took 24.2 s. The empirical reference
profile contains 180 isolated and 238 contention points, using 5 warmups and 20
samples, and took 18 min 25 s. The measured 96-thread endpoints were 30.219
TFLOP/s for the L1-hot executable GEMM service and 403.78 GB/s for NUMA-local
DRAM.

### Machine-local packed-B retention

Five independent processes paired distinct-address M12 scans with M120
ten-panel repeated scans on NUMA1 CPU 96. The stable fit excludes the noisy
0.5 MiB differential point and has 1.55 percentage-point RMSE from 1.25 MiB
upward.

- repeated-scan miss floor: 17.37% at the 1.25 MiB plateau;
- fitted retention knee: 0.662 x the nominal 2 MiB private L2;
- miss fraction at 2 MiB: 68.03%;
- miss fraction at 4 MiB: 79.80%.

| Calibration | Isolated MAPE | Contention MAPE | Contention P90 | Max selected regret |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Transferred retention prior | 7.95% | 11.14% | 16.97% | 9.21% |
| Machine-local retention | 7.96% | 10.85% | 16.08% | 9.21% |

The local probe improves absolute contention prediction but leaves plan
ranking unchanged. Packed-B retention is therefore a necessary physical
variable, not the missing ranking variable by itself. The declared 15%
contention-P90 and 5% maximum-regret gates remain open.

The narrow six-point analytical tile-window selector remains inside its 5%
selection gate after a 31-sample repeat of the boundary point: approximate
median/P90/max regret is 0.83/3.05/4.18%.

## Planner measurements

### High-skew diagnosis

The complete 2048-token TopK=6 high-skew trace was measured across fixed widths
and temporal orders.

| Plan | Measured | Model prediction |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| 8T reverse-even | 17.282 ms | 11.928 ms |
| 8T LPT | 17.814 ms | 11.932 ms |
| 16T reverse-even | 18.520 ms | 8.845 ms |
| 16T LPT | 22.779 ms | 8.819 ms |
| Full-planner selection | 22.857 ms | approximately 8.82 ms |

The measured-best 8T reverse-even plan is 24.43% faster in paired medians than
the full-planner selection, with P10/P90 gain of 22.01%/25.72%. Measured versus
predicted rank Spearman correlation over the manual candidates is 0.692. The
candidate space already contains the useful plan; the current failure is
cost-model ranking, especially underpriced wide-team concurrency and
temporal-order effects.

### Whole-expert versus two-stage execution

The strengthened two-stage artifact searched W13 and W2 widths independently
over 4/8/16 threads and included reverse-even/reverse-odd orders. It records
bitwise-equal outputs for every result. The best staged schedules use 8T for
W13 and 4T for W2, but remain slower than the best whole-expert schedule on all
three captured traces.

| Trace | Best whole expert | Best two stage | Two-stage delta | Paired P10 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| uniformish | 17.277 ms | 18.291 ms | +5.87% | +3.96% |
| median | 17.895 ms | 18.241 ms | +1.93% | +0.75% |
| high-skew | 17.745 ms | 18.454 ms | +3.99% | +2.91% |

Independent stage widths have real scheduling value, but on these traces they
do not repay the global W13-to-W2 barrier and full intermediate lifetime.
Two-stage execution remains a conditional planner candidate and mechanism
control, not the default.
